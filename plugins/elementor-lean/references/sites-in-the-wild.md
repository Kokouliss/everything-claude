# Elementor sites in the wild: what the fast ones do right and the slow ones do wrong

Measured 2026-10-15 with local Lighthouse 12 (mobile preset, Chromium, Node 22) on the home page of 32 public Elementor sites: Elementor's own properties, Elementor showcase winners, agency portfolios, Greek hotels, consultant "speed demo" sites, YouTuber sites, template-kit demos and WooCommerce shops. Single lab run per site (PSI/CrUX API returned HTTP 429 all day, so there is no field data). Scores range from **24 (janegoodall.org) to 96 (rometheme.net)**, median **49.5**; only 2 of 32 reach 90+, only 4 reach 80+, and 12 sit below 40. What separates the top from the bottom is not Elementor version (4.3.x at both ends), theme (Hello Elementor is 6 of the top 11 and 3 of the bottom 11), containers vs legacy sections (gapscout 88 with 26 sections; eloundapeninsula 28 with 330 containers), add-on presence or raw page weight (steffiedeleeuw 4.3 MB at 83; cloudcartel 1.7 MB at 31). Four numbers do: third-party bytes executed during load (top-third median about 25 KB, bottom-third median about 1.2 MB), Total Blocking Time (top median about 120 ms, bottom every site 1.0-6.5 s), hero type (top: text or a static `<img fetchpriority=high>`; bottom: 8 of 12 are autoplay video, background slideshow or Swiper slider, with LCP 9-24 s) and render-blocking head resources (the five sites with zero render-blocking resources all inline CSS and delay JS; bottom sites carry 0.8-2.9 s of blocking Google-Fonts-18-weight CSS, Typekit CSS, consent JS, jQuery and add-on CSS).

## What the fast ones do right

**Near-zero third-party JavaScript during load.** Top-10 third-party bytes: rometheme 0, gapscout 0, steffiedeleeuw 0, darrelwilson 16, websquadron 18, wickydesign 29, woolentor 114, speedifypress 117, element.how 168, livingwithpixels 420 KB (median about 25 KB). Bottom-11 median is about 1.2 MB. rometheme has gtag, AdSense, Google Sign-In and YouTube in its HTML and still shows 0 KB third-party in lab because WP Rocket holds them until first interaction. Lesson: third-party bytes executed before interaction is the strongest single predictor in the cohort, worth 15-25 points on the bottom sites.

**The hero is text or a static image with `fetchpriority=high`.** rometheme (H1, LCP 1.8 s), gapscout (h1, 3.6 s), steffiedeleeuw (h2, 3.5 s), element.how (35 KB PNG `<img>`, 2.8 s), websquadron (28 KB webp `<img>`, 3.3 s), woolentor (PNG `<img>`, 3.9 s), darrelwilson (PNG, 1.7 s). No top-6 site has a video, slideshow or slider in the first viewport. Lesson: the LCP element type decides the LCP range; text and small priority images land at 1.7-3.9 s, everything else at 5-24 s.

**Zero render-blocking resources via inlined/critical CSS plus deferred or delayed JS.** rometheme (WP Rocket: 0 external stylesheets, 61 of 62 scripts carry `data-rocket-src`), speedifypress (stylesheets=0), websquadron (PhastPress inlines CSS, JS as async XHR bundles), darrelwilson (Seraphinite), gapscout (Perfmatters + host aggregation) all report 0 items in `render-blocking-resources`; their FCP is 1.3-2.7 s against 3.0-7.1 s on the bottom sites. Lesson: the target is literally zero render-blocking resources, which combine-only optimizers never reach.

**Fonts are self-hosted woff2, 2-4 files.** gapscout 3 woff2 (127 KB), element.how 3 woff2, speedifypress 1 base64-inlined face, rometheme and websquadron Elementor local Google Fonts, steffiedeleeuw self-hosted; 8 of the top 10 make zero `fonts.googleapis.com` requests. Lesson: no external font CSS in the head, so no 0.8-1.0 s blocking request on the text LCP path.

**Hero and first-viewport images stay under about 50 KB.** element.how 35 KB PNG, websquadron 28 KB webp, gapscout 23 KB PNG mockup, woolentor 19 KB PNGs. Lesson: a small hero image is as fast as text; size, not format alone, is what matters at the top.

**Plugin and request counts are survivable when the main thread is idle.** rometheme has 62 script tags, WooCommerce and a full widget kit (DOM 2,200) at 96; steffiedeleeuw has four Crocoblock plugins, a 4.3 MB page and no cache plugin at 83; woolentor has 120 requests and 51 stylesheets at 79. Lesson: score the main-thread time, LCP path and third-party bytes before counting plugins, requests or kilobytes.

**What the fast sites still get wrong: icon fonts.** rometheme ships 657 KB of fonts in a 772 KB page (rtmicon .ttf 181 + 110 KB, eicons 106 KB, Font Awesome 156 KB); steffiedeleeuw eicons 105 + fa-brands 80 KB plus GT Super in eot/ttf/woff/woff2/svg; darrelwilson FA 155 KB; speedifypress loads fa-solid-900.woff2 (76 KB) even with Inline Font Icons on. Lesson: icon fonts are the remaining bytes problem at the top and the largest asset class on several slow sites (elara and moxcreative: 737-766 KB of icon packs).

**Lab variance is large and Delay-JS scores are the most fragile.** element.how 72 -> 93 on an identical payload; speedifypress 99 -> 80 when the delay payload fired inside the trace (12 req / 94 KB -> 62 req / 959 KB, TBT 746 ms); andreas-stricker 68 -> 39; livingwithpixels 83 -> 70; sophali 29 -> 54. Lesson: one run misclassifies about one site in four by a full tier; a 95+ target needs median-of-3 and script removal, not just deferral.

## What the slow ones do wrong

**Video or background-slideshow hero with no real poster.** janegoodall 24 (LCP is a `<video>` with a 1.76 MB PNG poster plus a 3.2 MB background mp4, 20.1 s), eloundapeninsula 28 (header container with background video, nothing paints, 15.2 s), hotelhub 32 (467 KB webm plus four more, 17.1 s), elementor.careers 34 (slideshow whose first slide is a 2,225 KB CSS-background webp, 21.8 s), pilot-beach 25, cloudcartel 31 (video + canvas + Lottie, 16.1 s). Mid-tier confirms it: elementor.com 68 (7 autoplay videos, 4.9 MB), wickydesign 70 (3.36 MB unpostered mp4), theartemispalace 68 (desktop 16:9 mp4 downloaded on mobile). Elementor's share: `background_background:video`, `elementor-background-slideshow` and autoplay `elementor-video` paint as CSS/video, not an `<img>`, so no srcset, preload or fetchpriority applies, and the desktop file is fetched on mobile. Lesson: a video hero caps the score around 35-70 regardless of everything else.

**Swiper hero with several full-resolution eager slides.** panorama-hotel 39 (three 793-959 KB slides, all eager with fetchpriority=high, H1 LCP 24.1 s), anendyk 35 (LCP is `div.swiper-slide-bg`, 1 MB JPEGs, 14 s), ccdigital 42 (carousel of unresized uploads, 11.7 s, CLS 0.83), chaniaboat 48 (zoom slider reserves no height, CLS 0.128, LCP 9 s). Lesson: slides render as background divs with `role=img`, so the browser cannot prioritise, and every slide loads; replace the mobile hero slider with one static image.

**Unoptimised uploads and CSS-background heroes.** stellaisland 31 downloads a 10,420 KB unscaled original; janegoodall a 1,757 KB PNG poster and 988 KB PNG logo; dragone a 566 KB PNG hero as CSS background and a 1,014 KB PNG hot-linked from i.ibb.co; eloundapeninsula 442 KB and 310 KB AVIFs plus a never-converted 366 KB PNG; brandstore, moxcreative, livingwithpixels and elara all have a container with a CSS background image as LCP element (moxcreative: 6.3 s LCP load delay). Lesson: Lighthouse reports `img_kb=0` for these pages because the images are backgrounds; audit from the network log and flag any first-viewport image over 200 KB, any photo saved as PNG, any `-scaled` or hot-linked URL.

**GTM fan-out into Facebook/TikTok/ad-tech with duplicate loads.** janegoodall GTM 852 KB / 2,624 ms blocking plus Facebook, Trade Desk, Bing, DoubleClick, AppNexus; eloundapeninsula two GTM containers, three gtag ids, G-BQ316965L4 loaded twice, Cookiebot, Facebook, Sojern (about 3.5 s blocking); stellaisland GTM 893 ms + Facebook 980 ms + TikTok; panorama AW-689848206 loaded twice; cloudcartel two full gtag bundles (GA4 + Ads); elementor.careers first-party GTM proxy still 165 + 165 + 158 KB. The profiler reported `facebook_pixel=false` on careers, panorama, brandstore and elounda; all four had a pixel via GTM. Lesson: pixels are invisible to HTML grep; count them from network entities, and treat a second GTM container or a duplicate gtag/reCAPTCHA load as a blocker.

**Cookie consent loaded synchronously in head, sometimes becoming the LCP.** eloundapeninsula Cookiebot uc.js render-blocking 1,532 ms; livingwithpixels 1,330 ms (FCP = LCP = 3.8 s on an otherwise lean page); janegoodall CookieYes 971 ms; pilot-beach's LCP element is `#CybotCookiebotDialogBodyContentText`; chaniaboat's is Complianz `.cmplz-message` at 9 s; element.how's biggest asset is iubenda core-en.js 112 KB. Lesson: CMP scripts must be async/deferred and the banner must not be an LCP candidate.

**Web-font chains: 18-weight Google Fonts, Typekit double CSS, TTF/EOT, mixed content.** Google Fonts requested with all 18 weight/style variants and `display=auto`: careers 783 ms, janegoodall 967 ms, stellaisland 843 ms, doorkit 888 ms, dragone 3 families x 18 = 54 variants 992 ms, woolentor 917 ms (the thing capping it at 79). Typekit's two render-blocking CSS files cost about 800-1,050 ms each on janegoodall, hotelhub, wickydesign and ccdigital. theartemispalace loads a 943 KB RobotoFlex .ttf alongside 5 Google CSS requests; andreas-stricker loads Satoshi-Variable.ttf twice. scopecopenhagen 65 and elara 43 reference local fonts over `http://` on an https page, so the fonts never arrive and the text LCP waits 6 s. brandstore loads 20 font files from 15 families because a ZipWP preview injection was left live. Elementor's share: the Kit enqueues every weight by default with `display=auto`. Lesson: external font CSS costs 0.8-1.0 s each on the LCP path; check scheme, format, weight count and host.

**Icon-font sprawl.** eicons 106 KB plus Font Awesome solid/brands/regular (76/80/13 KB) load on most sites; add-on packs add more: ElementsKit 454 KB .woff + Skyboot Material Design Icons 283 KB on elara and moxcreative (the two largest assets on each page), RomethemeKit .ttf icons 291 KB, Simple Line Icons, Font Awesome 4.7 loaded again by Custom Facebook Feed on anendyk. Lesson: budget zero icon-font files; Inline Font Icons (SVG) plus dequeue of unused packs.

**Render-blocking jQuery (and jquery-migrate) with 30-74 stylesheets, including when a cache plugin is installed.** dragone WP Rocket present, jQuery blocking 807 ms; cloudcartel jQuery excluded from the LiteSpeed combine, 604 ms; panorama LiteSpeed combines CSS but leaves jQuery 522 ms; anendyk WP-Optimize, 9 render-blocking files x 271 ms, 74 stylesheets; andreas-stricker 50 stylesheets, worst blocker is a honeypot anti-spam CSS at 780 ms; theartemispalace and sophali jquery-migrate blocking 554 / 319 ms. Six of the twelve bottom sites have an optimizer; none of the six defers JS. Lesson: "cache plugin present" is not a pass; check the `render-blocking-resources` audit after every change.

**Eager heavy third-party widgets.** dragone loads Google Maps JS synchronously in head with an empty API key (382 KB, 2,863 ms render-blocking) and reCAPTCHA twice (2 x 347 KB, 2.6 s blocking) for a below-fold form, plus about 1 MB of YouTube player JS; hotelhub reCAPTCHA twice via Gravity Forms; stellaisland reCAPTCHA + YouTube 1 MB + Triptease + TheHotelsNetwork + UserWay; anendyk YouTube 1 MB over 19 requests via EmbedPress + Instagram feed + certusonline booking; pilot-beach's abouthotelier booking React bundle is 1,142 KB and 5,609 ms blocking (87% of its TBT) plus Quicktext chat 462 KB; phlox Help Scout beacon 894 ms blocking. Elementor's share: Pro Form reCAPTCHA loads eagerly and the Video widget defaults to a live iframe without the image overlay. Lesson: no third-party script should execute before LCP; reCAPTCHA on form focus, YouTube behind a facade, booking/chat delayed to interaction, keyless Maps deleted.

**LCP hidden or painted by JavaScript.** cloudcartel's LCP is a `span.static-text` inside a rotating-text widget: render delay 15,498 ms (96% of LCP), 22.4 s main thread from a canvas `requestAnimationFrame` grid (3.8 s), two `setInterval(..., 0)` marquees (2.4 s), Lottie from unpkg and 32 entrance animations. hotelhub's hero paragraph waits 16,498 ms (96% render delay). andreas-stricker's text LCP has 5,828 ms render delay with 52 animated elements and Lenis smooth scroll. elara's 47 `elementor-invisible` elements contribute to a 6.7 s render delay. Agency data agrees: cretaforums 74 with 76 animations, voreniahorizon 50 with 160. Lesson: entrance animations set opacity:0 until frontend JS runs, and animated-headline widgets make the fastest hero type (text) the slowest; animations matter in volume (20+) and in the first viewport, otherwise they are a secondary lever.

**HTML and DOM bloat.** cloudcartel 1,105 KB HTML / DOM 5,032 (about 2 s of parse on a throttled phone); eloundapeninsula 880 KB / 3,426; andreas-stricker 626 KB / 2,320; panorama 542 KB including a 327 KB inline base64 image; elementor.com 586 KB with 33 stylesheets (FCP 2.0 s). Top sites: gapscout 46 KB document / DOM 641, element.how 33 KB / 783. rometheme is the exception (830 KB because WP Rocket inlines all CSS) and still scores 96. Lesson: large HTML tracks nesting depth, inline CSS print method and data-settings JSON, not sections vs containers; warn above 300 KB raw and 1,500 nodes.

**Add-on packs shipping global CSS/JS.** anendyk: HT Mega ships Bootstrap CSS globally, OSM Map's mapbox-gl.css and EmbedPress CSS render-block on a home page without a map; pilot-beach: UAE (particles.js) + Essential Addons + The Plus; eloundapeninsula: Royal Addons frontend.min.css 504 ms blocking; moxcreative: ElementsKit + JetElements/JetEngine/JetBlocks/JetSearch + WooCommerce = 62 scripts / 56 stylesheets; speedifypress: Royal Addons charts.min.js 71 KB / 987 ms CPU on a page with no chart. Lesson: one pack maximum, with conditional asset loading on, and dequeue on pages that use none of its widgets.

**Optimizer present but ineffective or fragile.** CSS-only combine leaves one 150-200 KB file blocking 1.3-1.5 s (chaniaboat, pilot-beach, phlox); concatenation keeps the full CPU cost (cloudcartel's 176 KB bundle = 8.45 s CPU; phlox 753 KB unused JS); Delay-JS gives +19 points only while its fallback does not fire (speedifypress); replacing WP Rocket with a CDN-only "optimizer" undid andreas-stricker's 2022 score. Lesson: prefer removal/dequeue for scripts the page does not need, keep delay for third parties, and verify three audits after any change: render-blocking-resources, TBT, request count.

**Negative result: build generation and Elementor version do not predict score.** Container-heavy bottom sites: eloundapeninsula 28 (330 containers), cloudcartel 31 (218), hotelhub 32 (91), careers 34 (104), andreas-stricker 39 (156). Section-only bottom sites: janegoodall 24 (38), panorama 39 (36), stellaisland 31 (22). Section-based fast sites: gapscout 88 (26 sections), speedifypress 80/99 (14). The three largest DOMs in the cohort are all container builds. Lesson: section-to-container migration is a layout risk with no measured score payoff; report Elementor version only for feature availability (Local Google Fonts, Inline Font Icons, Improved CSS Loading, Element Caching).

## Rules the plugin adopts from this

1. **Source of truth is the Lighthouse JSON network log, not HTML grep.** 7 of 32 profiles had empty or wrong static fields (WAF 403 pages on ccdigital, pilot-beach, elara, moxcreative, steffiedeleeuw, websquadron; GTM-injected pixels missed on 4 sites; SpeedifyPress, PhastPress, Seraphinite, WP Compress, Typekit and css2 font links undetected). Fetch HTML with a current mobile Chrome UA, detect 403/challenge pages, read versions from generator meta and `elementorFrontendConfig`, and attribute fonts, third parties, optimizers and add-ons from request URLs (`/plugins/<slug>/`, `data-rocket-src`, `speedify-spress`, `phast.php`, `seraph_accel`, `zapwp`, `use.typekit.net`, `fonts.gstatic.com`, `consent.cookiebot.com`).
2. **Run Lighthouse 3 times serially and report the median plus range**; record `benchmarkIndex` and discard runs below about 1,000. For Delay-JS sites also report the score with the delayed payload forced to execute.
3. **LCP element must be text or an `<img>`** with `fetchpriority=high`, `srcset`/`sizes`, explicit width/height and no `loading=lazy`. A `<video>`, a container with a background image or video, a `.swiper-slide*`/`.elementor-background-slideshow*` div, a cookie/consent element or an animated-headline span as LCP is a blocker.
4. **Hero rules on mobile:** no autoplay video (`background_background:video` or `<video autoplay>` in the first container); hero slider replaced by one static image; poster/hero image at most 100 KB AVIF/WebP, sized for 390-430 px; all non-hero `<video>` tags `preload="none"` with a poster; any Media resource over 500 KB in the network log is a finding.
5. **Image budget:** no first-viewport image over 200 KB, no page image over 300 KB, no PNG over 100 KB for photos or video posters, no `-scaled`/original size served to mobile, no image from a host other than the site origin (or its CDN), no base64 image over 20 KB in the HTML.
6. **Third-party budget:** at most 150 KB and at most 300 ms main-thread blocking before first interaction; one GTM container maximum, one gtag bundle, duplicate `gtag.js?id=` or `recaptcha__en.js` loads flagged; `connect.facebook.net`, `analytics.tiktok.com`, Hotjar, HubSpot, booking and chat scripts delayed to interaction or consent; a first-party GTM proxy is not credited.
7. **Eager vendor widgets:** reCAPTCHA loads on form focus only (and never twice); `maps.googleapis.com/maps/api/js` only on pages with a map and never with an empty `key=`; YouTube embeds use the Video widget image overlay or a facade (no `youtube.com/s/player` on load); any single third-party script over 300 KB or 500 ms blocking is a finding.
8. **Consent:** CMP script async/defer, loaded after first paint, under 30 KB; banner cannot be an LCP candidate (small type, positioned fixed, injected after paint); LCP element id/class matching `cookie|cmplz|Cybot|iubenda` is a blocker.
9. **Render-blocking-resources audit must be 0 items**; any item over 150 ms is a finding. jQuery and jquery-migrate inside the defer/delay set (verify they are not excluded by the optimizer); jquery-migrate and dashicons removed on the front end; a single combined CSS over 60 KB that still blocks is a finding; critical CSS inline, rest async; Elementor `css_print_method=external` with Improved CSS Loading on.
10. **Dequeue before delay.** Scripts the page does not use (swiper, charts, lottie, particles, Woo add-to-cart, elements-handlers on static pages, add-on frontend bundles) are removed per page; Delay-JS is kept for third parties only.
11. **Fonts:** Elementor Google Fonts set to Local (or self-hosted subset woff2), `font-display: swap`, at most 2 families and 4 files, about 150 KB total, LCP heading font preloaded. Findings: any `fonts.googleapis.com`/`use.typekit.net`/`p.typekit.net` request, a family string with 5+ weights or `ital`, any `.ttf`/`.eot`/`.svg`/`.woff` font, any `http://` font URL on an https page, the same font file fetched twice.
12. **Icon fonts: zero files.** Inline Font Icons (SVG) on; `eicons`, `fa-solid|brands|regular`, `elementskit*.woff`, `materialdesignicons`, `rtmicon`, `simple-line-icons` in the network log are findings; any icon font over 100 KB is a blocker.
13. **Animations:** none above the fold and none on the LCP element; more than 20 `elementor-invisible`/motion-fx elements per page is a finding; Animated Headline/typewriter as hero heading, Lottie, canvas rAF scripts, `setInterval` marquees and Lenis/smooth-scroll before LCP are blockers. Verify with the LCP phase breakdown: render delay under 40% of LCP.
14. **Main thread:** TBT under 300 ms and JS bootup under 1.5 s on the mobile run; concatenated bundles are judged by CPU time, not file count.
15. **HTML/DOM:** warn at 300 KB raw HTML or 1,500 nodes, finding at 600 KB or 2,500 nodes; flatten nested wrapper containers, remove hidden desktop/mobile duplicate sections, no `data:` URIs over 20 KB.
16. **Add-ons:** one add-on pack maximum with conditional asset loading; per-plugin asset bytes on the home page reported; packs whose widgets are absent from the page are dequeued; WooCommerce, CF7, dashicons and feed plugins only on pages that use them.
17. **Not performance levers (do not recommend as such):** section-to-container migration, Elementor version upgrades, "cache plugin installed", CSS/JS combining, first-party GTM proxying, CDN-only optimizers.

## Comparison with the agency's own sites

The agency's ten sites (27-86, median 68) sit inside the same range as the 32 public sites (24-96, median 49.5) and fail for the same reasons; the agency median is actually higher than the wild median, and no site on either side reaches 95.

- **pallada.gr 86** (LCP 2.5 s, 1,579 KB, 62 requests, 2 fonts, static hero, Perfmatters + W3TC) already matches the top pattern: static hero, two font families, delay/defer in place. Its gap to 95 is where element.how 93 and gapscout 88 sit too: residual render-blocking files, remaining plugin CSS/JS, LCP variance.
- **platonos dev 84, fagitiotiszois dev 68, kand dev 68** are in the steffiedeleeuw/woolentor/theartemispalace band, where the score is set by fonts, first-viewport image size and a handful of render-blocking files rather than by third parties.
- **cretaforums.gr 74 (76 entrance animations)** maps to the animation pattern (andreas-stricker 52 animations, elara 47, cloudcartel 32): animations alone are worth 5-10 points and matter most in the hero.
- **marakaki.com 74 (LCP 5.2 s with self-hosted fonts and only 49 requests)** matches the wickydesign/scopecopenhagen shape: a lean page whose LCP element, not its byte count, is the problem; check the LCP element type and its phase breakdown first.
- **voreniahorizon.gr 50 (160 animations, hero video)** and **onar-seaside-lounge.gr 44 (LCP 16.4 s, hero video, Premium Addons, 1,872 revisions)** are the autoplay-video pattern exactly as seen on janegoodall, eloundapeninsula, hotelhub, elementor.com and wickydesign; the single highest-leverage fix on all of them is a static poster `<img>` on mobile and `preload="none"` on every other video.
- **limnoupolis.gr 27 and staging 30 (three.js 3D hero, 5 GTM tags, 7 font families, LiteSpeed with all optimisations off)** hit every bottom pattern at once: a JS-painted hero (cloudcartel), tag fan-out (eloundapeninsula, stellaisland), font sprawl (brandstore, theartemispalace) and a present-but-inert optimizer (dragone, panorama). The wild cohort suggests the order of work: hero first (worth 15-30 points), tags second (15-25), fonts third (5-10), optimizer configuration last and only verified by the three audits.

The one thing the public cohort adds that the agency's own data could not show: the fastest public sites get their score by executing almost nothing before interaction, and the two 90+ sites are run-to-run unstable (element.how 72-93) or hold their score only while Delay-JS does not fire (speedifypress 99-80, rometheme 96 with 61 scripts held). A 95-100 target therefore means removing scripts and third parties from the page, not deferring them, and measuring with a median of three runs.

*Method notes: single local Lighthouse run per site on 2026-10-15 (mobile preset, Chromium, Node 22), no PSI/CrUX field data (API quota exhausted), no inner pages, sample size per pattern 2-11 sites; effect sizes are read from LCP/TBT breakdowns, not controlled experiments. Profiler HTML fields were wrong for 7 sites and have been corrected from the Lighthouse network log in the table above.*
